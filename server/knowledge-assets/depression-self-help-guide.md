# 抑郁自助导引 · Depression Self-Help Guide

> 本资料为循证心理健康教育，帮助你理解抑郁并尝试自助方法。它**不是诊断或治疗**，也不能替代专业人士。若症状持续、加重，或出现自伤念头，请尽快寻求专业帮助（见《危机资源卡》）。
> This is evidence-based psychoeducation to help you understand depression and try self-help strategies. It is **not a diagnosis or treatment** and does not replace professional care. If symptoms persist, worsen, or you have thoughts of self-harm, seek professional help promptly (see the Crisis Resources Card).

## 1. 抑郁是什么 · What depression is

抑郁症（重性抑郁障碍）是一种常见的、可治疗的医学状况，影响情绪、思维、身体与行为。它不是"想不开"或"意志薄弱"，而像糖尿病、高血压一样，是身心交互作用下的健康问题。

Depression (major depressive disorder) is a common, treatable medical condition that affects mood, thinking, body and behavior. It is not "weakness" or "thinking wrongly"; like diabetes or hypertension, it is a health condition arising from mind-body interaction.

常见表现 · Common features：
- 持续情绪低落、空虚或易怒 · persistent low mood, emptiness or irritability
- 对以往喜欢的事失去兴趣或乐趣（快感缺失）· loss of interest or pleasure (anhedonia)
- 睡眠改变（失眠或嗜睡）、食欲/体重改变 · sleep and appetite/weight changes
- 疲劳、精力不足、动作或思维变慢/坐立不安 · fatigue, psychomotor slowing or agitation
- 注意力下降、犹豫不决 · poor concentration, indecisiveness
- 无价值感、过度自责、绝望 · worthlessness, excessive guilt, hopelessness
- 反复想到死亡或自杀 · recurrent thoughts of death or suicide

## 2. 为什么会得抑郁 · Why it happens

抑郁通常由多因素共同作用：遗传与体质、大脑神经递质与神经可塑性变化、重大生活事件与慢性压力、早期经历、躯体疾病（如甲状腺问题）、以及酒精/药物等。理解"这是多因素的健康问题"有助于减少自责。

Depression usually results from multiple interacting factors: genetics and temperament, changes in neurotransmitters and neuroplasticity, major life events and chronic stress, early experiences, medical conditions (e.g., thyroid problems), and alcohol/drugs. Understanding it as a multi-factor health problem helps reduce self-blame.

## 3. 循证自助方法 · Evidence-based self-help

以下方法有研究支持，适合轻中度症状或作为专业治疗的补充：

### 3.1 行为激活 Behavioral activation
抑郁让人退缩，退缩又加重抑郁。行为激活的核心是"先行动，动机随后而来"：有计划地安排能带来**成就感**或**愉悦感**的小活动，从很容易的开始，逐步增加。见配套《行为激活日程表》。

Depression drives withdrawal, and withdrawal deepens depression. The core of behavioral activation is "act first, motivation follows": schedule small activities that bring **mastery** or **pleasure**, starting very easy and building up. See the companion Behavioral Activation Schedule.

### 3.2 认知重构（CBT）Cognitive restructuring
抑郁常伴随自动化的负性想法（如"我什么都做不好"）。CBT 帮助你识别这些想法、检验其证据、形成更平衡的看法。见配套《思维记录表（CBT）》。

Depression often comes with automatic negative thoughts (e.g., "I can't do anything right"). CBT helps you notice these thoughts, examine the evidence, and form a more balanced view. See the companion CBT Thought Record.

### 3.3 规律作息与睡眠 Regular routine and sleep
固定起床与睡觉时间、白天接触自然光、限制卧床清醒时间、睡前减少屏幕与咖啡因。睡眠与情绪高度相关。

Keep regular wake/sleep times, get daylight exposure, limit time awake in bed, and reduce screens and caffeine before sleep. Sleep and mood are strongly linked.

### 3.4 身体活动 Physical activity
规律运动（如每周数次、每次 20–40 分钟的快走）对轻中度抑郁有帮助，效果可与心理治疗互补。从"能做到的量"开始。

Regular exercise (e.g., brisk walking 20–40 minutes, several times a week) helps mild-to-moderate depression and complements therapy. Start with what you can manage.

### 3.5 社会联结 Social connection
抑郁会让人孤立自己。主动、小剂量地恢复联系（一条消息、一次短暂见面）能缓冲低落情绪。

Depression pulls people into isolation. Proactively restoring contact in small doses (a message, a brief meetup) buffers low mood.

### 3.6 正念与放松 Mindfulness and relaxation
正念练习、腹式呼吸、渐进式肌肉放松有助于降低反刍思维与生理紧张。每天 5–10 分钟即可开始。

Mindfulness, diaphragmatic breathing, and progressive muscle relaxation reduce rumination and physiological tension. Even 5–10 minutes daily is a start.

### 3.7 限制酒精与避免药物滥用 Limit alcohol, avoid substances
酒精是中枢抑制剂，短期"麻痹"、长期加重抑郁与睡眠问题。

Alcohol is a depressant: it numbs briefly but worsens mood and sleep over time.

## 4. 何时寻求专业帮助 · When to seek professional help

- 症状持续 **两周以上**，影响工作、学习、家庭或社交。
- 自助方法尝试后无明显改善，或持续加重。
- 出现**自伤或自杀念头**——请立即求助（见《危机资源卡》）。
- 伴随幻觉、妄想，或曾有过度兴奋、精力异常旺盛、睡眠需求减少的时期（可能提示双相障碍）。

Seek help if: symptoms last **more than two weeks** and affect functioning; self-help isn't working or things worsen; you have **thoughts of self-harm or suicide** (act immediately — see the Crisis Resources Card); or you experience hallucinations, delusions, or past periods of abnormally elevated mood/energy with reduced need for sleep (possible bipolar disorder).

专业帮助包括：心理治疗（如 CBT、人际治疗 IPT、行为激活）、药物治疗（抗抑郁药，需由医生评估开具与随访），以及二者联合。中重度抑郁通常建议专业治疗。

Professional help includes psychotherapy (CBT, interpersonal therapy, behavioral activation), medication (antidepressants, prescribed and monitored by a doctor), or a combination. Moderate-to-severe depression usually warrants professional treatment.

## 5. 帮助身边的人 · Supporting someone with depression

倾听而不急于"给建议"或说"想开点"；肯定其感受；鼓励并陪同寻求专业帮助；关注安全（见危机卡）；也照顾好自己。

Listen without rushing to "fix" or saying "cheer up"; validate their feelings; encourage and accompany them to professional help; watch for safety (see crisis card); and care for yourself too.
