# 思维记录表（CBT）· CBT Thought Record

> 认知行为疗法（CBT）认为：影响我们情绪的，往往不是事件本身，而是我们对事件的**解读**。抑郁时，大脑会自动产生偏负面的想法。本表帮助你**识别 → 检验 → 平衡**这些想法。
> CBT holds that what affects our mood is often not the event itself but our **interpretation** of it. In depression, the mind generates automatically negative thoughts. This sheet helps you **notice → examine → rebalance** them.

## 使用步骤 · Steps

1. **情境 Situation**：什么时候、在哪里、和谁、发生了什么？（客观描述）
2. **情绪 Emotion(s)**：你感到什么？给强度打分（0–100%）。
3. **自动想法 Automatic thoughts**：脑中闪过的念头是什么？哪一句最"扎心"（热想法 hot thought）？
4. **支持/反对证据 Evidence**：有哪些事实支持这个想法？有哪些事实**不支持**它？
5. **平衡的想法 Balanced thought**：综合证据后，更客观、更友善的说法是什么？
6. **重新评分 Re-rate**：现在再给最初的情绪强度打分，看看变化。

1. **Situation**: when, where, who, what happened? (objective)
2. **Emotion(s)**: what did you feel? Rate intensity (0–100%).
3. **Automatic thoughts**: what went through your mind? Which is the "hot thought"?
4. **Evidence for/against**: what facts support the thought? What facts **don't**?
5. **Balanced thought**: weighing the evidence, what is a more objective, kinder statement?
6. **Re-rate**: rate the original emotion again and notice the change.

## 常见认知扭曲 · Common cognitive distortions

| 扭曲 Distortion | 含义 Meaning | 例子 Example |
|---|---|---|
| 非黑即白 All-or-nothing | 只看极端、没有中间地带 | "不完美就是失败" |
| 过度概括 Overgeneralization | 由一次推及永远 | "这次没做好→我永远做不好" |
| 心理过滤 Mental filter | 只盯负面、忽略正面 | 只记得一句批评 |
| 贬低正面 Discounting positives | 把好事说成"不算数" | "那只是运气" |
| 读心/算命 Mind reading / fortune telling | 断定他人想法或未来 | "他们一定觉得我很差" |
| 灾难化 Catastrophizing | 把事情想到最糟 | "一点失误就会毁掉一切" |
| 情绪化推理 Emotional reasoning | 用感受当证据 | "我觉得没用，所以我真没用" |
| "应该"陈述 Should statements | 用"应该"逼自己 | "我应该 always 坚强" |
| 贴标签 Labeling | 给自己/他人贴死标签 | "我是个废物" |
| 个人化 Personalization | 把不属于自己的责任揽上身 | "都是我的错" |

| Distortion | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| All-or-nothing | only extremes, no middle | "If not perfect, it's failure" |
| Overgeneralization | one event → always | "I failed once → I'll always fail" |
| Mental filter | fixate on the negative | remember only one criticism |
| Discounting positives | good things "don't count" | "that was just luck" |
| Mind reading / fortune telling | assume others' thoughts or the future | "they must think I'm awful" |
| Catastrophizing | imagine the worst | "one slip ruins everything" |
| Emotional reasoning | feelings as evidence | "I feel useless, so I am" |
| Should statements | pressure with "should" | "I should always be strong" |
| Labeling | fixed negative labels | "I'm a loser" |
| Personalization | taking undue blame | "it's all my fault" |

## 填写示例 · Worked example

- **情境**：给朋友发消息，两小时没回。
- **情绪**：失落 70%、焦虑 50%。
- **热想法**："他一定是不想理我了，我让人讨厌。"（读心 + 贴标签）
- **支持证据**：确实还没回复。
- **反对证据**：他平时会晚回；上周我们还聊得很开心；他可能在工作/没看手机；我并没有做错什么。
- **平衡的想法**："没及时回复更可能是他忙，而不是讨厌我。就算真有矛盾，也可以之后沟通，不代表我这个人令人讨厌。"
- **再评分**：失落 30%、焦虑 20%。

- **Situation**: texted a friend, no reply for two hours.
- **Emotion**: sad 70%, anxious 50%.
- **Hot thought**: "They must not want to talk to me; I'm unlikeable." (mind reading + labeling)
- **Evidence for**: no reply yet.
- **Evidence against**: they often reply late; we had a great chat last week; they may be working or away from their phone; I did nothing wrong.
- **Balanced thought**: "A delayed reply is more likely about their being busy than disliking me. Even if there's an issue, we can talk later — it doesn't make me unlikeable."
- **Re-rate**: sad 30%, anxious 20%.

## 小贴士 · Tips

- 想法 ≠ 事实。把它当作"一个假设"去检验，而非真理。
- 对自己用你会对好朋友说的、同样友善而现实的话。
- 每天 1 条即可，贵在坚持与复盘。

- A thought is not a fact. Treat it as a hypothesis to test, not a truth.
- Speak to yourself as you would to a good friend — kind and realistic.
- One entry a day is enough; consistency and review matter most.
