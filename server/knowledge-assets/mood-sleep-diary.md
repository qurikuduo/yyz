# 情绪与睡眠日记 · Mood & Sleep Diary

> 记录能帮你和治疗师看清情绪、睡眠、活动与想法之间的规律。抑郁常伴随睡眠紊乱与活动减少，规律记录本身就是干预的一部分。
> Tracking helps you and your clinician see patterns between mood, sleep, activity, and thoughts. Depression often disrupts sleep and reduces activity — the act of recording is itself part of treatment.

## 如何使用 · How to use

1. 每天固定时间填写（例如睡前）。Fill in at a fixed time each day (e.g., before bed).
2. 情绪用 0–10 打分：**0 = 极度低落，10 = 状态很好**。Rate mood 0–10: **0 = extremely low, 10 = doing well**.
3. 记录睡眠的实际时长与质量，而非"应该睡多久"。Log actual sleep duration/quality, not how much you "should" sleep.
4. 至少坚持 1–2 周，再回顾规律。Keep it up for at least 1–2 weeks, then review the patterns.

## 每周回顾 · Weekly review

- 本周情绪平均分 / 最低分出现在哪一天？发生了什么？
  Average / lowest mood this week — which day, and what was happening?
- 睡眠时长与情绪是否有对应关系？睡不好的第二天情绪如何？
  Does sleep duration track with mood? How is mood the day after poor sleep?
- 哪天活动量或运动较多？那天情绪有没有不同？
  On days with more activity/exercise, was mood different?
- 有没有让你情绪明显变好或变差的固定情境、人或习惯？
  Any recurring situations, people, or habits that clearly lift or lower mood?
- 下周想尝试的一个小改变是什么？（如固定起床时间、白天散步 15 分钟）
  One small change to try next week? (e.g., fixed wake time, a 15-minute daytime walk)

## 睡眠卫生要点 · Sleep hygiene basics

- 固定起床时间（比固定入睡时间更重要）。Keep a consistent wake time (matters more than bedtime).
- 床只用于睡眠，不在床上刷手机/工作。Reserve the bed for sleep — no phone/work in bed.
- 若躺下 20 分钟仍清醒，起身到昏暗处做放松的事，有困意再回床。If awake after ~20 minutes, get up, do something calm in dim light, return when sleepy.
- 下午后减少咖啡因，睡前减少酒精（酒精会破坏后半夜睡眠）。Limit caffeine after midday; avoid alcohol before bed (it fragments later sleep).
- 白天规律活动、适度光照；睡前减少强光与屏幕。Get daytime activity and light; dim screens/lights before bed.

## 何时把记录带给专业人士 · When to bring this to a clinician

- 情绪持续两周以上低落、兴趣明显减退。Low mood and reduced interest lasting 2+ weeks.
- 睡眠严重紊乱（几乎不睡或过度嗜睡）。Severe sleep disruption (near-insomnia or hypersomnia).
- 出现自伤或自杀念头——请立即联系危机热线或就医（见《危机资源卡》）。
  Any self-harm or suicidal thoughts — contact a crisis line or seek care now (see the Crisis Resources Card).
